## Photos

Files are in `img/bg/`, with thumbnails in `img/thumb/`. Every photo was cropped to 16:9 and
resized to 1920x1080. For each there is also `<name>-glass.jpg`, a blurred, brightened copy used
as the scenery clock's fill, and for some `<name>-fg.webp`, the same photo with its sky cut away;
both are derived from the photo and carry its licence. Settings, Backgrounds shows each
photographer's name beside the photo.

### Scenes from couch-tv

`scene-night.jpg`, `scene-golden.jpg`, `scene-water.jpg`, `scene-fog.jpg` and `scene-dawn.jpg`,
with their `-fg.webp` cut-outs and `-glass.jpg` copies, were made for couch-tv with an AI image
model from text only. They are not real places. couch-tv's credits say: "Use them however you like."
